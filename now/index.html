How this is computed

Each planet’s position comes from JPL’s long-range Keplerian elements (the Standish tables, fitted for 3000 BC – 3000 AD), solved in your browser at the instant shown. That is the same data and the same solver as the 3D orrery, so the two always agree. Positions are geometric: light-travel time and aberration are not applied, and the figures are good to a few arcminutes for the present day. Times are UTC.

These are astronomical positions, not a horoscope: no signs, no houses, no predictions. For a professional-grade ephemeris use JPL Horizons.

Questions

Which planets can I see tonight?

The summary at the top names them for the current moment. Planets listed under the evening sky are east of the Sun, so they are above the horizon after sunset, in the general direction the Sun went down; those under the morning sky rise before the Sun. A planet more than 150° from the Sun is near opposition and up most of the night. Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are naked-eye objects; Uranus needs binoculars and Neptune a telescope.

What does “retrograde” mean in the table?

Apparent retrograde motion: for a few weeks or months around opposition a planet appears to drift backwards (westward) against the stars, because Earth is overtaking it on the inside — or, for Mercury and Venus, because they are overtaking Earth. The planet never actually reverses; it is a line-of-sight effect. The table checks each planet’s geocentric longitude a day either side of now. It has nothing to do with astrology.

How accurate are these positions?

Good to a few arcminutes for the present day, drifting to a fraction of a degree at the ends of the 3000 BC – 3000 AD range. Distances are centre to centre. This page is an educational tool, not a navigational or scientific reference.

Can I see where the planets were, or will be, on another date?

Yes — the 3D orrery takes any date and time from 3000 BC to 3000 AD, plays time forwards or backwards, follows any planet, and jumps straight to famous alignments from its events menu.
